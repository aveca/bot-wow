How it works

Enter your own coordinates

Add the fishing spots you want to compare. BOT-WOW cannot see your character or discover your location.

Review the proposed order

The route is ordered using straight-line Euclidean distance in the coordinates you enter. It is a planning aid, not in-game pathfinding.

Calculate from prices you observed

Enter buy price, sell price and quantity. The margin is calculated with this formula:

Margin: (sell − buy) × count

Clear limits

BOT-WOW is a browser information tool. It does not connect to World of Warcraft, capture the game screen, send clicks, move your character, read live Auction House prices or verify catch rates. The demo uses synthetic data. The companion interface is currently in English; no downloadable package is offered.
