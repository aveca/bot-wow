Not this product — better to say it now

BOT-WOW does one job: fishing. If you want mob grinding, herb mining, combat rotations or PvP, this is the wrong tool, and no discount would change that.

  • Three further roles are in development. The module ledger lists each one with its real status and the tests behind it
