Fishing planning guide

Plan a WoW fishing route from spots you choose

A useful route starts with places you have recorded yourself. BOT-WOW helps you organise those coordinates, compare a suggested stop order and calculate a margin from prices you have actually observed.

Important: BOT-WOW is a manual browser planner. It does not connect to World of Warcraft, discover in-game locations, read your character or move it. You enter the coordinates and prices; you follow the route yourself.

When a manual fishing route planner helps

If you already keep notes about fishing spots, a route planner can make those notes easier to compare. Instead of deciding from memory each time, you can enter the places you want to visit, give each one a name and review a geometric order before you start.

This approach is useful when you want to organise a short list of familiar locations, compare a few possible stops or keep your own notes alongside the route. It is not a live map of Azeroth and it cannot tell you whether a spot is currently safe, reachable or productive.

How to plan a route in four steps

  1. Record the spots yourself

    Write down a name and a pair of coordinates for each place. Use one consistent coordinate system for the whole list. BOT-WOW does not read the game map or collect locations for you.

  2. Enter your coordinates

    Add the coordinates to the browser companion. The planner compares straight-line Euclidean distances between the numbers you entered; it does not calculate an in-game path.

  3. Review the proposed order

    Use the suggested order as a planning aid. Terrain, water, flight paths, transport, obstacles, server rules and changing conditions are outside the geometric calculation, so use your own judgement.

  4. Follow the route manually

    Keep the route visible, then travel and fish yourself. The companion does not capture the game screen, send clicks or control a character.

Calculate margin from prices you observed

If you want to compare the economics of a fishing session, enter the buy price, sell price and quantity yourself. BOT-WOW uses only those inputs:

Margin = (sell price − buy price) × quantity

It does not read live Auction House prices, supply, demand, catch rates or sale history. Check the market yourself and update the numbers when they change.

What BOT-WOW does not do

  • It does not connect to a World of Warcraft client or addon.
  • It does not capture the game screen or read process memory.
  • It does not send clicks, move a character, cast a line or automate gameplay.
  • It does not discover fishing spots or provide live Auction House prices.
  • The public demo uses simulated data, not real catches or measured in-game results.

Common questions

Does BOT-WOW connect to World of Warcraft?

No. It is a browser information tool and uses coordinates that you enter yourself.

Does it find fishing spots automatically?

No. You choose the places and enter their coordinates manually.

Does it use live Auction House prices?

No. The margin calculation uses the buy and sell prices you type in.

Does it move or click for my character?

No. You follow the proposed route and play manually.
