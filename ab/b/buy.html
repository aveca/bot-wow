Built for the way you actually fish

Three profiles, one tool. Whoever you are, BOT-WOW keeps the repetitive part predictable instead of exhausting.

The routine player

You fish the same hours every week and want the same result every time. Spot memory and optimised rounds give you a repeatable loop, not a lucky one.

The efficiency chaser

You measure your gold per hour. Nearest neighbour + 2-opt on travel time, mob risk and failure odds turns a wandering route into a planned one.

The alt collector

You rotate characters or realms. The spot map exports and imports as JSON, so a proven round is reusable instead of relearned.
