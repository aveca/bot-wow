BOT-WOW - fishing route planner

Version 1.2.0 · plan your own route, compute your margin, stay in control.

What it is

BOT-WOW orders a walking route between the fishing spots you choose and computes a margin from your own buy and sell prices: (sell - buy) x count. You enter the spots and prices yourself.

What it is not

It does not read World of Warcraft, it does not capture the game window, it does not click or inject anything, and it does not automate gameplay. No downloadable package is offered.
