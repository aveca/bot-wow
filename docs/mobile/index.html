What this page is

Mobile companion

You type every coordinate and every price. The page orders a walking route and computes a margin on the numbers you type. It does not read the game, does not click, and sends nothing to a server.

Route, margin and journal are calculated on this device and stored in this browser only. Blizzard's EULA prohibits bots; nothing here automates World of Warcraft.

        What works offline

        Installed, this page opens without a network: spots, route, margin and journal are computed and stored on the device. Service status, offers and documentation links cannot be refreshed offline, and the status box is then marked "cached" with the time it was read.

        ! Offline. You are looking at the cached copy. Route, margin and journal stay on this device; status and offers are not refreshed.

        Install on iPhone or iPad

        1. Tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar.
        2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
        3. Confirm the name, then tap Add.

        On iOS the page then opens without the browser bars, offline, with the icon you see here.
