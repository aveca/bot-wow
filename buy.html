What it does not do (yet, or ever)

  • Spot-to-spot movement is still in validation. The browser demo is simulated and does not prove in-game movement.
  • No combat, XP farming, quests or battleground automation. Those are planned, with no release date.
  • Pixel reading reduces the technical footprint but guarantees nothing.

Before you buy

Can I subscribe or preorder now?
No. These are proposed plan prices only. Monthly billing, cancellation, installation, activation and customer delivery have not all been verified, so checkout and paid reservations are closed.
How will a monthly plan renew and cancel?
Monthly auto-renewal with online cancellation is the proposed model. It is not available yet, and no billing terms are being accepted on this page.
Does it work on my client?
Compatibility of the packaged release has not been verified on a clean installation. Check the compatibility page for the currently documented scope; the proposed plans are not available yet.
Is there a free trial?
The browser demo runs the planner on simulated data, with no account and no install. It does not demonstrate the Windows build or game interaction.
Can I get a refund?
For an existing purchase, read the refund policy or contact support. The proposed monthly plans are not open for purchase.
Can I use it on two computers?
Device limits for the proposed plans are not implemented. Existing lifetime licences remain honoured; contact support for an existing licence question.
Will I get banned?
Automation breaks Blizzard's terms of service and can get your account suspended or banned. That risk is yours, and no tool removes it.
