Frequently asked questions

Straight answers. If a question is missing, write to support@bot-wow.com.

What exactly does the bot do?
It fishes, the way a careful human would: it casts, watches the float with calibrated computer-vision checks, clicks when the bite arrives, and keeps looping. It memorises every spot it visits and computes optimised rounds to keep them in rotation. It is a fishing-only product: no combat, quests or battleground automation.
Is there a free trial?
Yes — the browser demo runs the real planner on simulated data, with no account and no install. The bot itself is a paid product; we do not ship time-limited binaries.
How much does it cost?
€20 once, for life. No subscription, no renewal, no seat limits beyond one machine per licence. The full list of what is included is on the pricing section of the home page.
Which payment methods do you accept?
Card payments through Mollie's checkout. A manual bitcoin lane exists (you contact support, payment is verified, then an admin issues the licence explicitly). If card payment is temporarily unavailable, the purchase button tells you rather than pretending — and a waitlist lets us notify you.
Can I get banned for using it?
Automation tools can violate a game server's terms of service, and intensive automation is detectable through behaviour analysis. We do not promise safety we cannot prove. Use an account you are prepared to lose — the compatibility page covers this in detail.
Does it work on private servers?
That is our reference environment and it is tested there, on Classic 1.13. Retail and other Classic versions are not tested and therefore not supported.
Can it move my character between spots?
Partially. The odometer and movement controller exist and pass unit tests, but full spot-to-spot travel is still being validated in game; the statuses on the features page are kept accurate as this progresses.
What happens if my character dies?
Today: the bot stops safely and asks for a human to handle the corpse run. An automated recovery is planned and being implemented — but until it proves itself in game, we keep the safe stop.
My antivirus flags the executable / what it does.
Screen-capture and synthetic-input utilities sometimes trigger heuristics. The install guide documents exactly which Windows APIs the bot calls, and the distribution package carries a SHA-256 manifest so you can verify what you received.
How do updates work?
Updates of the 1.x branch are included in the lifetime licence; they ship as new versioned packages with release notes. Receipts: license keys are re-sent by support on request.
Refunds?
A refund can be requested within 14 days of purchase as long as the licence has not been activated on a machine, plus persistent-download failure and double-charge cases. The full procedure is on the refund policy.
