What is BOT-WOW today?
BOT-WOW is a browser companion for organizing spots and prices that you enter yourself. Free includes 4 spots; Planner 9,00 € (reference one-time price) includes 12 spots and route export; Studio 29,00 € (reference one-time price) includes 40 spots, named lists and a catch journal saved in this browser. These prices do not mean that new checkout is open.
Does it connect to World of Warcraft or automate play?
No. It does not read a game process or window, move a character, fish, or send clicks. Nowhere as a bot: it is not intended to automate official or private servers. You follow any route yourself.
What do I enter, and how are results calculated?
You enter spot names and coordinates. For margin calculations, you enter buy price, sell price and count; the formula is (sell − buy) × count. The tool does not provide live Auction House prices, catch rates or game telemetry.
Can I try it before buying?
Yes. The public browser demo uses simulated data and needs no account or installation. It is not a trial connected to a game.
