Frequently asked questions

The licence is 20,00 € once. Questions about an order: support@bot-wow.com.

What does BOT-WOW do?
It fishes, remembers the schools, plans the round, walks from spot to spot, and comes back after a death. It reads the game window as pixels and sends clicks as window messages. Tested in real conditions.
Where was it tested?
In real conditions, on a live client. The public browser demo is a separate preview of the planner, on simulated data.
What do I get for 20,00 €?
A lifetime licence: the fishing loop, school memory, the route planner in three modes, spot-to-spot travel and corpse recovery. One payment, no renewal.
How do I pay?
Enter your email on the buy page and continue to card checkout. The price is fixed at 20,00 EUR on the server.
When do I receive the download?
After the payment provider confirms the payment, the licence and the download link go to the email you entered.
Does the browser demo connect to a game?
No. The demo lets you inspect the planner on simulated data. The Windows assistant is what reads the game window.
Refunds?
The published refund policy allows a request within 14 days if the licence has not been activated. Read the refund policy before you pay.
