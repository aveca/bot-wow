Refunds

A refund can be requested within 14 days of purchase, as long as the software has not been activated on a machine.

Qualifying cases

  • The software does not run on an environment that matches the documented prerequisites.
  • The download persistently fails after three attempts.
  • The payment was charged twice for a single order.

Procedure

  1. Write to support@bot-wow.com with the order email address and the payment date.
  2. The refund is processed within 7 business days, back to the original payment method.

An already-activated licence cannot be refunded, except for a proven and unfixable technical defect. Support answers before any decision.
